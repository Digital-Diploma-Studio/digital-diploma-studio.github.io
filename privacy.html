Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Digital Diploma Studio ("we", "us") sells digital study flashcards through our Etsy shop (etsy.com/shop/DigitalDiplomaStudio) and shares study content on Instagram and Pinterest. This policy explains what personal information we handle and why. Contact: diggitymedia@hotmail.com

What we collect

Email subscribers. If you sign up for our free sample or mailing list, we collect your email address (and your first name if the form asks for it). This is collected by our email provider on our behalf.

Etsy purchases. When you buy from our shop, Etsy processes your order, payment and any details you give. We see only what Etsy shows shop owners, such as your name and order details. We do not see your full payment details.

Messages. If you message us through Etsy, Instagram, Pinterest or email, we keep the conversation so we can reply.

Website and app data. We do not run our own website that tracks visitors. Etsy, Instagram, Pinterest and our email provider may use their own cookies and analytics under their own policies.

How we use it

  • To send the free sample and any study content you asked for.
  • To email you about new decks, only if you subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave at any time.
  • To answer your questions and fulfil orders.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run the shop and the email list (Etsy, our email provider, and our email and file storage providers).

Our Pinterest and Instagram tools

We use a private tool, connected through the Pinterest and Instagram APIs, to publish posts and pins to our own accounts. It works only with our own accounts and content. It does not collect, store or process personal information about other Pinterest or Instagram users.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep email subscriber details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Security

Our accounts are protected with passwords and two-factor authentication where available, and access tokens are stored securely on our own computer. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Medical content

Our study cards are for education only and are not medical advice. This does not affect how we handle your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
